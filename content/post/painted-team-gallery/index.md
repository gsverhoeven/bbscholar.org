# September 2026: Imperial Nobility

<img src="{{< blogdown/postref >}}teams/imperial_nobility.jpeg" alt="" width="672" />

With the arrival of Third Season, I really wanted to play this team. As the models all look similar with their plumes, I decided to go all in on "positional readability".
The color scheme was almost entirely picked from the contents of the Army Painter Speedpaint Starter Set 2.0, except for the yellow color.
Painted during the summer months of 2026, ready for league start in September. Playing them now as "Heeren van 's Graveland".

# March 2026: Tomb Kings

<img src="{{< blogdown/postref >}}teams/tomb_kings.JPG" alt="" width="672" />

This is the infamous GW metal 5th edition Khemri team, consisting of many small metal components that need to be filed, drilled, and glued together (a process which I actually really enjoyed).
Assembly started in December 2023. Started painting skellies, but got stuck.
In their unpainted state these were known in the B.I.G. League as Osiris Oldhammerers.
Picked these up again almost two years later with Third Season in December 2025, and finished them in March 2026.
Followed up with an Undead Blitzer to serve as Ivan Deathshroud Star player.
They are now known as the Kootwijkse Zandstuivers.

# January 2026: Snotlings

<img src="{{< blogdown/postref >}}teams/snotling.jpeg" alt="" width="672" />

I could not resist getting this plastic GW box, with two pump wagons. For the two trolls I picked the classic 3rd ed Ripper and 2nd ed Troll Bork Bulge Belly.
Started in February 2025. Finished in January 2026. Played in the B.I.G. league as "Skitter Support team" .

# December 2025: Norse

<img src="{{< blogdown/postref >}}teams/norse.jpeg" alt="" width="672" />

This 3rd edition metal GW team was mostly bought already painted by a french coach "noplicastic" in October 2024.
I really love his paint job, several models are converted to more dynamic poses, and the use of wingless GW Chaos Furies as Ulfwereners is really unique and works for me.
I completed the roster in November 2025 with a metal Warhammer 6th ed Ogre Kingdoms Yhetee and two Punga beer pigs.
December 2025 saw the finishing touch with the extra 3rd edition blitzer added, that did NOT have his ridiculously large horn cut off.
Team name: "Norse Boys".

# September 2025: Amazons

<img src="{{< blogdown/postref >}}teams/amazons.JPG" alt="" width="672" />

For this team I bought three boxes of 4th edition metal amazons and split these with another coach to get sufficient models.
This was the big skin tone experiment, as well as a color picking challenge.
And Jort taught me how to make my own tufts! Painted during the most of 2025. 
Played in the B.I.G league as "Cobracopana Punchers", and managed to win a Sevens tournament with them.

# Feb 2025: Chaos Chosen

<img src="{{< blogdown/postref >}}teams/chaos_chosen.jpeg" alt="" width="672" />

This team was bought online already painted. It combines 2nd edition chaos players painted by "Albert" with 5th edition (nurgle) beastmen.
Played them as "Chaos, Bloody Chaos" (yes that is a Sepultura SO) at a few tournaments.

# December 2024: Gnomes

<img src="{{< blogdown/postref >}}teams/gnomes.JPG" alt="" width="672" />

In March 2024 GW surprised us all and introduced garden gnomes into Blood Bowl. I completed the roster with a 2nd edition Treeman.
Started assembly in May, completed them in December 2024. Played them in the B.I.G. League as "Corversbos Gnomes".

# September 2023 Lizardmen

<img src="{{< blogdown/postref >}}teams/lizardmen.JPG" alt="" width="672" />

Bought in august 2021. Assembled in february 2022, started with painting in November 2022. 
Introduction to modern GW plastic, GW contrast paints and static grass basing. The Fanath Big Lizard model is perfect as Kroxigor for this team.
Played at a few tournaments under such names as "Run Skink MC - Tougher than Leather" or "Ancestral Resurrection" to appeal to their dino vibe.

# August 2021 Humans

<img src="{{< blogdown/postref >}}teams/humans.jpeg" alt="" width="672" />

Painted in 2020 during COVID-19, this team brought me back into the hobby.
Added two metal 2nd edition Human models as blitzers. And a 2nd edition Human Ogre.
This was also the team I took to the Alicante World Cup in 2023, and did awful with them, but had a great time nevertheless.
